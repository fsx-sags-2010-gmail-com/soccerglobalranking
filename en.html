About Soccer Global Ranking

Soccer Global Ranking (SGR) brings together information about clubs and competitions in a single structure to make football data easier to consult, compare, and follow.

Methodology

SGR uses sporting data and calculation procedures to produce a comparative measure of team and competition strength. The goal is to present information clearly, consistently, and in an accessible format.
