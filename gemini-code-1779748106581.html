OFFICIAL GLOBAL FORMULA

WELOR = 300 + (20 × Global Club Index)

Each club contains dozens of variables meticulously calculated using six structural index weights:

  • Historical Strength (35%): Historical consistency, tradition, and multi-era stability.
  • Recent Performance (25%): Modern competitiveness and short to medium-term strength.
  • Titles Weight (15%): Trophy relevance across international, continental, and national cups.
  • Continental Strength (10%): Continental competitive level and difficulty calibration.
  • National Strength (10%): Domestic league difficulty and depth of the country's ecosystem.
  • Competitive Value (5%): Technical potential, structural quality, and financial competitiveness.

THREE-LAYER RATING STRUCTURE

To avoid short-term distortions while remaining dynamically alive, the platform processes three distinct intelligence layers to compile the final professional rating model:

  • 1. WEEKLY DYNAMIC ELO: Represents live football form, recent results, short-term competitiveness and current live momentum.
  • 2. MONTHLY STRUCTURAL INDEX: Represents long-term structural strength, national relevance, and continental positioning.
  • 3. HISTORICAL LONG-TERM BASE: Permanent relevance and legacy that protects traditional clubs from temporary distortions.
OFFICIAL FINAL RATING MODEL: 70% Structural WELOR + 30% Dynamic Weekly Elo
